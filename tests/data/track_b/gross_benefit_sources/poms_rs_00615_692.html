RS 00615.692 Computing the Amount of the Delayed Retirement Credits (DRC)

To view the monthly credit for each possible DRC as both a fraction and as a decimal equivalent, see the chart in RS 00615.692D in this section. Only use the decimal equivalent for estimates. The fractions shown represent the part of one percent each credit is worth. For example, 2/3 percent means 2/3 of one percent. The chart also shows the total credit possible per year.

To view how the change in full retirement age (FRA) affects the total possible increase that may apply for any date of birth (DOB) see the chart in RS 00615.692E in this section.

 

A. Basis for computing DRCs

Add DRCs to the primary insurance amount (PIA) if the number holder (NH) did not receive a reduced benefit prior to FRA. If the NH received a reduced retirement insurance benefit (RIB) for any month before FRA, add the DRCs to the monthly benefit amount (MBA).

B. Interactive Computation Facility (ICF) for DRCs

Use the ICF to process benefit payment computations with DRCs. For additional instructions on computing DRCs, refer to MSOM ICFT2 002.005.

C. Formula for computing DRCs

1. To add DRCs use the following formula:

  • •

    MBA times the number of DRCs times .01 times the fraction equals the credit

  • •

    Round this amount down to the nearest ten cents and add it to the MBA to determine the new benefit amount. (Prior to 06/82 round up.)

2. Computation errors with the decimal equivalent or multiplication sequence

While these charts show both fractions and decimal equivalents, it is possible, because of rounding, that the decimal will sometimes result in an answer that is 10 cents less than the answer resulting from use of the fraction. For that reason, use the decimal equivalent only for estimates. Always use the fraction to compute benefits. Decimal equivalents are only included to assist in providing estimates.

For example:

PIA = $1000.00

Date of birth = 06/15/1940

30 DRCs apply at 7/12 of 1 percent per month

The correct method to use:

$1000.00 times 30 times .01 times 7/12 = $175.00

$1000.00 + $175.00 = $1175.00

Using the decimal equivalent, results in an incorrect amount.

$1000.00 times 30 times .0058333 = $174.999 ($174.90 after rounding down)

NOTE: ICF program #1 will compute delayed retirement credits. Instructions are in MSOM ICFT2 002.001.
