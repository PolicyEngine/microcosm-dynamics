# Urban 2010 Track A v2 rulings

## d603 — structural pre-count weighted arithmetic, 2026-09-28

Max's ruling d603 clarifies §16.7 item 7 of `a2-ratified-1`:

> No bar on weighted arithmetic … The structural pre-count runs the projection normally, weighted DI transitions included; it outputs only the frozen §16.7 counts. 'No weight sums' in the spec default applied only to that pre-registration peek's outputs, never to registered weighted estimates or budget totals.

Max also stated:

> we need to estimate total budgetary impacts for sure!

The structural path therefore runs the unchanged projection, including its
internal weighted arithmetic. Its outputs remain limited to D-unsupported
histories, S ordering classes, S-refused earlier spells and opening-proxy
applications, together with the frozen protocol and attempt metadata. It
emits no benefit amounts, weight sums, weighted totals or tabulations.
The separate frozen protocol and pre-execution record required by §§11 and
16.7 remain prerequisites to any real structural run.

This ruling does not change the ratified specification's bytes. A change to
the ratified document requires a new version. The output restriction on this
pre-registration structural check does not prohibit registered weighted
estimates or estimates of total budgetary impacts.
